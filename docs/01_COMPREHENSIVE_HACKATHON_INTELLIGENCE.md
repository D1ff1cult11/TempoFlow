## 4. Colosseum Judging Criteria & Scoring Rubric

Colosseum evaluators score submissions across 5 distinct pillars:

```
[1. Product & Technical Execution] ───► 30% : Live deployed contracts, clean architecture, test coverage, sub-second UX
[2. Business & Market Potential]  ───► 25% : Massive TAM, clear monetization model, real commercial upside
[3. User Experience & Design]     ───► 20% : Elite UI, zero wallet friction, responsive mobile-ready web app
[4. Innovation & Defensibility]   ───► 15% : Novel protocol primitive, proprietary logic, high barriers to entry
[5. Pitch, Video & Presentation]  ───► 10% : Under 3-minute crisp video, clear README, architecture diagrams
```

### What Disqualifies or Tanks Projects:
- ❌ **Non-functional demos**: Broken wallet connectors, mock buttons that do nothing, or requiring judges to run 15 CLI steps locally to test.
- ❌ **Generic "AI wrappers"**: Prompting ChatGPT via an API route and calling it "Decentralized AI" (judges instantly reject these).
- ❌ **Uninspired forks**: Copying Uniswap V2 or Pump.fun with reskinned CSS.
- ❌ **Over-bloated scope**: Claiming to build 10 features, where all 10 are half-baked. It is 10x better to build **one killer flow** that works flawlessly.
