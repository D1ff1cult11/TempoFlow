## 1. How the Multi-Track Architecture Works

In Colosseum's multichain Arena, projects are evaluated across:
1. **An Ecosystem Sponsor Track** (e.g. Tempo, Hyperliquid, Zcash, Solana)
2. **A Global Horizontal Category** (e.g. Payments & Stablecoins, DeFi, DePIN, Infra)
3. **A Secondary Sponsor or Special Award Track** (e.g. Cross-Chain Settlement, Privacy Shield, Public Goods, University)

### The Golden Rule of Hackathon Multi-Tracking:
> **Never build three separate mediocre projects. Build ONE world-class protocol that naturally bridges two ecosystems and solves a high-priority vertical problem.**

When judges from Sponsor A review your project, they see a deep, native integration of their L1 primitives.  
When judges from Sponsor B review your project, they see a bridge connecting their users to new liquidity.  
When category judges review your project, they see a polished, production-grade business solving a multi-billion dollar problem.
