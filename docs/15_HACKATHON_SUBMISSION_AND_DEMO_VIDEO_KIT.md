### 4. Problem Statement
> *"Stablecoins settle trillions, yet retail point-of-sale and global payroll remain broken due to three critical roadblocks:*
> *1. **The Gas Token Tax**: Forcing shoppers to hold volatile native tokens (ETH/SOL) to make a $10 stablecoin purchase causes 95% checkout drop-off.*
> *2. **Execution Volatility**: Shared blockchains prioritize high-fee MEV bots during volatility, leaving retail checkouts waiting 20-60 seconds at the counter.*
> *3. **Cross-Border FX Friction**: Paying global contractors requires manual DEX swaps with 0.3% fees, slippage, and bank wire latency.*
> *Businesses and workers need sub-second, fixed-cost, stablecoin-native settlement."*

### 5. Detailed Solution & How You Built It
> *"TempoFlow is built as a native financial primitive on Tempo L1 with Solana cross-chain ingress:*
> *- **Stablecoin-Native Gas**: Native fee abstraction enables gas payments directly in USDC/EURC.*
> *- **Dedicated 0.6s Payment Lanes**: Sub-second deterministic settlement with fixed $0.001 fees.*
> *- **Enshrined AMM FX**: Zero-slippage currency conversion executed at the consensus validator level.*
> *- **Continuous Living Payroll**: Mathematical linear streaming ($O(1)$) where wages tick upward every second and can be claimed on-demand.*
> *- **Non-Custodial Settlement Vault**: Multi-currency treasury with automated revenue split rules.*
> *- **Universal Solana Ingress**: Shoppers pay with 1 click from Phantom, relayed into Tempo settlement vaults.*
> *- **Zero-Barrier Judge Demo Mode**: Evaluators test full end-to-end payment flows without needing faucets, external wallets, or testnet gas."*
