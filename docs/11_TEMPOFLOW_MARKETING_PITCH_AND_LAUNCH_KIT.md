**Post Body**:
```markdown
Hey everyone,

Over the past few weeks building for Colosseum's Crypto World's Fair, our team focused on a frustrating paradox in crypto:

Stablecoins process trillions of dollars in annual volume, but if you try to use USDC for real-world merchant checkout or international contractor payroll, the UX collapses:
1. The "Gas Token Tax": You have $100 in USDC, but you can't buy a $10 item because you lack $0.50 of the chain's volatile native gas token.
2. Network Spikes: When trading surges, merchant POS terminals freeze.
3. Outdated Batch Payroll: Remote contributors in emerging markets wait 14-30 days for SWIFT wires and lose 3-7% on predatory FX spreads.

### What We Built: TempoFlow
TempoFlow is an open-source, programmable settlement protocol designed around the new Tempo blockchain (incubated by Stripe & Paradigm) and connected to Solana retail liquidity:

- **Stablecoin-Native Gas**: Transactions and gas fees are paid directly in USDC/EURC. Users never need to hold or calculate volatile gas tokens.
- **Dedicated Payment Lanes**: Sub-second (0.6s) deterministic settlement with fixed $0.001 fees that never spike during volatility.
- **Enshrined AMM FX Engine**: Instant currency conversion between USD and EUR stablecoins at the validator layer with near-zero slippage.
- **Continuous Living Payroll**: Mathematical $O(1)$ salary streaming. Workers watch their salary tick up every second and can withdraw accrued earnings anytime.
- **Cross-Chain Solana Ingress**: Shoppers pay with 1 click from Phantom or Solana Blinks, settling directly into the merchant's Tempo vault.
- **ZK Audit Receipts**: Employees and employers can generate cryptographically verified income certificates for tax compliance without leaking balances publicly.

We built an interactive "Judge / Testnet Demo Mode" right into the web terminal so you can test customer checkout and streaming payroll without needing testnet faucets.
```

### Problem Statement:
> Traditional blockchain payments fail real-world commerce due to the "Gas Token Tax" (forcing consumers to acquire volatile gas tokens to spend fiat-backed stablecoins), latency volatility during network congestion, and the lack of continuous streaming infrastructure for modern global contractor payroll.

### Solution:
> TempoFlow leverages Tempo L1's payments-first architecture to provide stablecoin-native gas, dedicated 0.6s payment lanes, and validator-enshrined AMM currency conversions. Connected to Solana's massive retail base via cross-chain ingress and Blinks, TempoFlow delivers gasless merchant checkout and real-time by-the-second streaming payroll with zero per-tick gas overhead.

### Technical Architecture & Integrations:
> 1. **Tempo L1 Smart Contracts**: Written in Solidity ^0.8.24 (`TempoFlowRouter.sol`, `TempoPayrollStream.sol`), featuring native stablecoin fee abstraction and Enshrined AMM integration.  
> 2. **Solana Ingress Program**: Written in Rust/Anchor (`tempo_crosschain_ingress`), accepting retail SPL-Token deposits and emitting cryptographic payment intent events.  
> 3. **Decentralized Relayer**: Bridges cross-chain events to Tempo L1 settlement vaults in under 1.5 seconds.  
> 4. **Frontend**: Next.js 15, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Viem, and Solana Wallet Adapter, featuring a real-time salary odometer and interactive Judge Simulation Mode.

### Business Model & Future Roadmap:
> Monetization via a 10 bps protocol settlement fee and 5 bps Enshrined FX spread. Post-hackathon, TempoFlow will pilot with Web3 DAOs and freelance marketplaces, integrate Stripe's Machine Payments Protocol (MPP) for AI agent streaming, and apply for the Colosseum Accelerator.
